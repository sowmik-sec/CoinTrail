# Taste
- Likes a structured "grilling" workflow before building: wants exhaustive Q&A covering tech stack through every spec and functionality before implementation starts. Confidence: 0.85
- Answers scoping questions concisely in numbered prose matching the questioner's numbering (q1: ... q2: ...), and freely picks options rather than deliberating at length. Confidence: 0.75
- Prioritizes data durability and longevity in personal tools: data must survive phone loss/phone changes and last 10+ years; chose Google Drive backup over local-only storage for this reason. Confidence: 0.9
- For data safety prefers belt-and-suspenders over minimalism: chose every backup mechanism offered (CSV export, full backup/restore file, automatic periodic backup, cloud backup) rather than picking one. Confidence: 0.75
- Prefers flexible, user-customizable structures over rigid fixed lists (e.g., categories that can be added/renamed/hidden; customizable payment-method list), and consistently picks the fuller-featured option over the minimal one: per-category budgets in addition to overall budget, month-over-month per-category comparisons, full Google Drive API integration over the simpler file-picker backup, real two-device sync over last-backup-wins, auto-generated recurring expenses over manual logging, and an 80% budget warning on top of the 100% alert. Confidence: 0.85
- Currency/locale: BDT (৳) — Bangladesh Taka, amounts formatted in ৳. Confidence: 0.95
- Builds personal-use apps Android-first with Kotlin + Jetpack Compose and Material 3, and values daily reminder notifications as the driver for everyday habits like logging expenses. Confidence: 0.75
- Even when building an app for himself first, wants it architected to later share with friends and family (per-account data isolation from day one, distribution-ready) rather than a single-user dead end. Confidence: 0.8
- Prefers English-only UI in his own apps; other-language localization (e.g. Bangla) is a non-goal unless he says otherwise. Confidence: 0.7

Confidence: 0.7
