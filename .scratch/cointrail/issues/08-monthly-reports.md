# 08: Monthly reports

**What to build:** A month view where each day is a cell in a calendar grid showing its total (heatmap-style intensity) and tapping a day opens that day's list. Below it: the monthly total, a per-category breakdown as horizontal bars (donut available as a switch), month-over-month comparison with total delta and per-category deltas versus the previous month, and budget progress bars (overall + per category).
